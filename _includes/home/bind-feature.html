BIND: painting baryons onto dark matter

A conditional flow-matching model that maps dark-matter-only halos to their full hydrodynamical counterparts — dark matter, gas, and stars — across the 35-dimensional IllustrisTNG parameter space, in about a second per halo. Two papers submitted, models and generated halos released, and a live explorer running inference on a cloud GPU.
